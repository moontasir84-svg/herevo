HerEvo

Most apps answer her questions. HerEvo gets her ready for the room where her rights are decided.

Rural women rarely lose land, dower or safety for lack of information. They lose them in five moments: a paper they can't read, the day land is divided, the hard conversation, the office desk, and the night nobody wrote down. HerEvo is built for those moments. Voice first, in Bangla.

  1. Paper LensPhotograph any paper. It reads it aloud, highlights dates and amounts, and says "do not sign" when a paper gives her land away.
  2. My ShareExact Islamic-law shares, computed (not guessed), drawn as her field. Shows what is being kept from her.
  3. RehearsalThe AI plays the brother-in-law, the bribe-asking clerk or the salish chairman. A coach scores every line.
  4. WhisperAt the real desk: what the official said, the truth, and the one sentence to say back.
  5. WitnessA PIN-locked diary sealed with a hash chain on her phone, turned into a dated statement for a protection order.
